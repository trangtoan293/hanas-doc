## Iceberg — khong co

Logo Apache Iceberg la huy hieu tron chua tang bang chuyen sac xanh. Ep ve mot mau
thi ra hinh tron dac, khong nhan ra duoc. Khong tim thay ban don sac chinh chu
(iceberg.apache.org chi co Iceberg-logo.svg va .png ban mau).
Dung chip chu cho Iceberg, hoac nhung ban mau goc lam ngoai le.

## Thuong hieu

Logo Apache (Kafka, Spark, Airflow, NiFi, Ranger, Polaris, Superset) la nhan hieu
cua ASF. Dremio, DataHub, Dify la nhan hieu thuong mai. Dung kieu "powered by"
thuong thuoc nominative use — nhung day la trang thuong mai, can phap che duyet
truoc khi len production.
